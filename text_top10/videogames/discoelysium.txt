Disco Elysium is such an amazing game.
There really isn't a lot of gameplay. The diaglogue is the gameplay. It is more of a very interesting interractive novel, which consists of cool prose (which sometimes can feel very *itallic english* which I like).
The writing is definetly it's strongest point and that is shown in the characters and world building, just your sidekick Kim Kitsuragi has so much character and is very likeable. It is very obvious that Robert Kurvitz spent a long time building the world of Elysium and it feels dense, rich, interesting and unique. It feels like Disco Elysium just scratches the surface of what Elysium really is, which makes it such a shame, that we probaly never will get some kind of sequel or any kind of "continuation" of Elysium, since the IP is in such a limbo due to differences between the creators and ZAUM.
Not only is the writing amazing, the atmosphere is on another level. Paired with the soundtrack from Sea Power, makes the "feeling" of Disco Elysium feel something I really can't describe. It might be nostalgia? Walking around in Revachol, I get the feeling of the cold winter days, where you wake up in the morning, but don't have to go to school/work that day. I feel comfortable, when playing Disco Elysium.
The coolest thing about Disco Elysium is the main characters inner monologue, which has the different parts of your psyche talking for you and also sometimes having a back and forth with each other, which works so wonderfully.
